# CHALK - Tactical Board

An interactive football tactics board for grassroots coaches, by Shaun Ellison.

- Animated set pieces
- Squad management
- A library of 32 plays across 96 phases
- Works fully offline, with everything saved on your own device

CHALK is shared free within Turton FC and Shaun's other club. It is not for public sale or redistribution; the licence (EULA) is shown inside the app on first use.

## How to open it

1. Download `index.html`.
2. Open it in Chrome, Edge or Safari, on a phone, tablet or computer.

No internet connection or installation is needed.

## Your saved work

Boards, plays and squads are saved in the browser on the device you use. They do not move between devices, and clearing the browser's data deletes them.
